# Welcome

Hello, and welcome to my blog!

This first post is a short introduction to who I am, why I decided to start writing, and what I hope to build here over time. Think of it as the starting point for everything that follows.

My name is **Zerui Zhao**. I am currently pursuing an undergraduate degree in **School of Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU-SAI)**, with a growing interest in becoming a scientific researcher and making contributions to real life.

I am still exploring where my interests will lead, but I enjoy learning by building things, asking questions, and turning unfamiliar ideas into something I can explain clearly.

## Why I Started This Blog

Learning often feels complete when a lecture ends, a book is closed, or a program finally runs. In reality, recognizing an idea is very different from understanding it well enough to explain and use it.

Writing gives me a way to test that understanding. To write clearly, I have to organize scattered thoughts, identify gaps in my knowledge, and decide what really matters. A blog therefore becomes more than a collection of notes: it is a record of how my thinking changes.

I started this blog for three main reasons:

1. **To learn through output.** Explaining an idea is one of the best ways to discover whether I truly understand it.
2. **To document progress.** Small lessons are easy to forget, but written notes make growth visible over time.
3. **To communicate better.** Regular writing can improve both technical explanation and everyday expression.

## What I Will Write About

The topics will probably evolve with me. For now, I expect the blog to include:

- **Learning notes** — concepts worth revisiting and explanations written in my own words.
- **Technical projects** — what I build, the decisions I make, and the problems I encounter.
- **Tools and workflows** — useful software, development practices, and lessons from experimentation.
- **Reflections** — occasional thoughts on study, growth, and life beyond code.

I do not expect every post to be perfect or final. Some will be polished articles; others may simply capture a useful idea while it is still fresh. When a post is unfinished, I will label it clearly and continue improving it.

## Looking Ahead

I do not have a rigid publishing schedule. My goal is more modest and, I hope, more sustainable: keep learning, write when I have something worth organizing, and improve each post a little more than the last.

Over time, I hope this blog becomes:

- a reliable archive of what I have learned;
- a place where projects and ideas can develop in public;
- evidence of progress, including the mistakes along the way;
- and, occasionally, something useful to another reader.

This is only the beginning. Thanks for visiting, and I hope you will find something worthwhile here.
